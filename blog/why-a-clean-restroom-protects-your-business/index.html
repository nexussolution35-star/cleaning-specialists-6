The number that should worry every business owner

94% of visitors say they would avoid a business after encountering a dirty restroom. Your washroom is part of your brand — and a poor one quietly sends customers to your competitors.

It's not just perception

Poor hygiene drives absenteeism. The SA economy loses around R12 billion a year to absenteeism, much of it preventable with proper cleaning and disinfection of high-touch areas.

What good washroom hygiene looks like

Serviced sanitary bins, hand hygiene (soap, sanitiser, paper towel), toilet hygiene (seat sanitisers, in-line sanitisers), air-care and scheduled deep cleaning — installed, serviced and documented.

Make it someone else's job

Our hygiene teams inspect, install and service your washrooms on a schedule, with records you can show an auditor. Book a free assessment and we'll scope it for you.
