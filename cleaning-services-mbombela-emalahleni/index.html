Two Regions, One Standard

Mbombela (Nelspruit) in the Lowveld and eMalahleni (Witbank) on the Highveld are Mpumalanga's two biggest commercial centres, and we run a local branch for each. Businesses with sites in both, franchises, retailers, logistics operators along the N4, get the same documented standard from the same group, coordinated centrally.

Multi-Site & Corporate Cleaning

If you manage premises in more than one town, we quote and schedule across both regions at once: one scope, one standard, one point of contact, with service certificates per site.

Local Teams, Not Travelling Crews

Work in Mbombela is done by our Nelspruit-based team and work in eMalahleni by our Highveld team, so you get local response times without sacrificing consistency.

Local Questions

Cleaning in Mbombela & Emalahleni, Answered

We have branches in both Nelspruit and Witbank, can you service both?

Yes. That's exactly what this combined service is for: one scope and standard across both regions, delivered by the local branch in each.

Who actually does the cleaning in each town?

Our Nelspruit branch covers greater Mbombela and the Lowveld; our Highveld branch covers eMalahleni, Middelburg and surrounds. Both are locally run.

Do you offer a single invoice for multi-site contracts?

Yes. Multi-site contracts can be consolidated with per-site service records, ask for this in your free assessment.
