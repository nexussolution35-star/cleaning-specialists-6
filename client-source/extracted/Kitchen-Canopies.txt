Specialist Cleaning: Kitchen Deep Clean

For the safe preparation, handling, and serving of food, your 
commercial kitchen needs to comply to rigorous Health & Safety 
standards. 

We deep clean your kitchen thoroughly, safely and systematically

All electrical items are isolated and exposed switches covered with a 
waterproof tape or polythene cover

All Kitchen Equipment, where practical, are dismantled, cleaned, 
sanitised and re-assembled by trained operatives

Where possible, equipment is pulled out to ensure that thorough 
cleaning can be achieved both beneath and behind the equipment

Mechanical and chemical cleaning are applied (green option 
available)

Walls and ceilings are normally steam cleaned, before the 
equipment is returned into position

Cooking and preparation areas are sanitised

Floor areas are thoroughly cleaned with use of a ﬂoor scrubber, 
steam cleaner and wet dry vacuum cleaner

Skirtings, corners, pipework, legs and wheels are inspected and 
re-cleaned if necessary, all rubbish and debris is removed and area 
is tidied
