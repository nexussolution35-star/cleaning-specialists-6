For Businesses

Commercial solar panel systems are typically larger in number and more complex than 
residential systems which is why you should rather consider professional solar panel cleaning. 

Professional cleaners have the expertise, equipment, and safety protocols necessary to clean 
commercial solar panels effectively and safely ensuring that the panels are cleaned properly 
without causing damage. This is important for businesses looking to maximize their energy 
production and return on investment. 

For Home

When it comes to home solar panels: dust, dirt, bird droppings, and other debris can 
accumulate on the panels over time, reducing their efficiency. 

A build-up of grime can block sunlight from reaching the solar cells, which in turn can lower 
the amount of electricity the panels produce. 

By keeping the solar panels clean, you can ensure that they are operating at maximum 
capacity, which in turn prolongs their lifespan and maximise their energy savings ability.
