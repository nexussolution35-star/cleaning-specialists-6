Payment FAQ

Do you require a deposit?

No. We do not collect deposits. Scheduled contracts are billed monthly; once-off cleans are settled on completion once you have walked the work.

How is a contract priced?

On area, frequency, surfaces and compliance load, not a flat per-desk figure. Your quote itemises scope per area so you compare like with like.

Can you handle multi-site billing?

Yes. Multi-site retail and commercial portfolios receive consolidated billing and per-site documentation.

What about HACCP and audits?

Tailored programmes include service certificates and treatment records designed to keep your facility audit-ready every day.
