Questions

Frequently Asked

How fast can you assess my premises?

We respond quickly. In most areas a consultant will walk your site and provide a written scope within the same week, at no charge.

Do you handle compliance and HACCP environments?

Yes. Whether it is a home with children and pets, an HACCP food facility, a hospital or a warehouse, we match products and protocols precisely to your environment.

Are you registered and insured?

We are FASA, SAPCA and NCCA registered and fully insured across South Africa. Service certificates and records are standard on every job.

Are you a local team or a national call centre?

Every Specialists branch is owner-managed and run by a dedicated owner-director who has personally invested in their business and carries full accountability.

What guarantees do you provide?

Full documentation on every job, without exception. Service certificates, treatment records and written guarantees are your compliance file and your audit trail.
