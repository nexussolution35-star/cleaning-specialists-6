Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you handle both commercial and domestic cleaning?

Yes. We tailor commercial cleaning for offices, factories and large operations, and flexible domestic cleaning for every area of your home.

Which areas around Nelspruit and the Highveld do you cover?

We cover a wide footprint across the Lowveld (from White River to Komatipoort) and the Highveld (from Middelburg and Witbank to Secunda and Ermelo). See our Service Areas page for the full list.

Are you compliant for food and healthcare environments?

Yes. We use safe, proven, tested methods and match products and protocols precisely to HACCP food facilities, hospitals, warehouses and homes.

Are you registered and insured?

We are FASA, SAPCA and NCCA registered. Service certificates and records are issued as standard, not on request.

How do I get a quote?

Call your nearest branch or send us a message and we'll arrange a free, no-obligation site assessment and written quote.
